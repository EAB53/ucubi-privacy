Ucubi: Last Haven — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Ucubi: Last Haven ("the game") is developed by Emirhan Askin Bak.

The game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

  • No accounts, no sign-in. You never enter personal information.
  • No analytics, no advertising SDKs, no tracking. The game contains no third-party SDKs for ads or analytics.
  • Progress stays on your device. Your Soul Tree progress and settings (sound, vibration, tutorial seen) are saved only in the game's local storage on your device. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the game deletes them.
  • No internet required. The game works fully offline.
  • Beta testing. If you test the game through Apple TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and the feedback you choose to send with the developer, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Children. The game does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

If this policy changes (for example, if ads or online features are added in the future), this page will be updated and the change will be announced in the app's release notes.

Contact: please open an issue at github.com/EAB53/ucubi-privacy/issues.
